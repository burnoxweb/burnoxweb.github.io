FAQ, short mein.

Video kitne time mein milega?

Usually 24–48 ghante mein. Zyada reels ya lamba video ho to time pehle hi bata deta hoon.

Payment kaise hoga?

UPI se (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm). Pehle watermark wala preview dekho, pasand aaye to pay karo, phir final file milti hai. Website ke liye thoda advance lagta hai.

Kya milne aana padega?

Nahi. Sab WhatsApp pe ho jaata hai: video bhejo, preview dekho, final lo. Tatisilwai / Ranchi mein ho aur milna chaho to bhi theek hai.

Changes karwa sakte hain?

Haan, final se pehle 2 chhote changes free hain (text, gaana, cut). Bada change ho to WhatsApp pe baat kar lenge.

Website ka .com mil sakta hai?

Haan. .com domain dukaan ke apne naam pe khareeda jaata hai, taaki hamesha aapka hi rahe. Domain ka kharcha (lagbhag ₹500–1000 saal ka) alag hota hai, setup main kar dunga.

Kaunsi video bhejun? Quality kaisi ho?

Phone ki normal video chal jaayegi. Bas roshni achhi ho aur clip hilti na ho. WhatsApp pe "document" bana ke bhejoge to quality best rahegi.

Aapka agla reel viral ho sakta hai

Abhi WhatsApp karo. Video bhejo, preview lo, pasand aaye tabhi pay karo.
